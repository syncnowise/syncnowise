# Syncnowise Digital Hub

Create a modern, professional, and visually attractive website for a software company called "Syncnowise".

COMPANY OVERVIEW:

Syncnowise is a software development company that provides Website Development, Mobile App Development, SaaS Platform Development, and UI/UX Design services. We help startups, businesses, and enterprises build scalable, high-quality digital products with clean design and reliable engineering. Our target audience is startups, growing businesses, and enterprises looking for a trusted technology partner.

DESIGN STYLE:

- Modern, clean, minimal, premium SaaS-style design (similar to Stripe, Linear, or Vercel)

- Dark mode friendly with a gradient accent color scheme (blue/purple gradients)

- Smooth animations and micro-interactions on scroll and hover

- Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop

- Modern sans-serif font (Inter or Poppins)

- Rounded cards, soft shadows, glassmorphism touches, and generous white space

PAGES / SECTIONS NEEDED:

1. Navbar - "Syncnowise" logo, links (Home, Services, About, Portfolio, Pricing, Contact), a "Get Started" CTA button, sticky on scroll, collapses into a hamburger menu on mobile

2. Hero Section - Bold headline like "Building Smart Software Solutions for Tomorrow" with subheading about Syncnowise's expertise, CTA buttons ("Get Started" and "View Our Work"), and an animated illustration/graphic on the right representing software/app development

3. Services Section - Grid of 4 cards with icons for:

   - Website Development

   - Mobile App Development

   - SaaS Platform Development

   - UI/UX Design

   Each card should have a short description and a "Learn More" link

4. About Us Section - Short company story about Syncnowise's mission to deliver innovative, scalable software, plus animated stat counters (e.g., "50+ Projects Delivered", "30+ Happy Clients", "5+ Years Experience", "100% Client Satisfaction")

5. Portfolio/Projects Section - Showcase of past web, app, and SaaS projects in a card/grid layout with hover-zoom effects and category tags (Web / App / SaaS)

6. Process/Workflow Section - Steps showing how Syncnowise works (Discovery → Design → Development → Deployment → Support) in a horizontal timeline or numbered cards

7. Testimonials Section - Client reviews in a carousel/slider with client photo, name, company, and rating stars

8. Pricing Section - 3-tier pricing cards (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) with a highlighted "Most Popular" plan and feature checklists

9. Team Section - Team member cards with photo, name, role, and social links

10. Contact Section - Contact form (Name, Email, Phone, Message, Service Interested In - dropdown), company email, phone, address, and social icons

11. Footer - Syncnowise logo, quick links, services list, social media icons, newsletter subscription, and copyright "© 2026 Syncnowise. All rights reserved."

FUNCTIONALITY:

- Smooth scroll navigation between sections

- Scroll-triggered fade-in/slide-in animations

- Fully responsive layout for mobile, tablet, and desktop

- Working contact form with validation

- Hover effects on buttons, cards, and links

- Optimized loading performance

Make the overall design feel trustworthy, innovative, and tech-forward — a design that reflects Syncnowise as a reliable, modern software development partner.
